AIFRED VST 3.6-Beta

A real-time mix analysis tool built to help you understand what your mix is actually doing while you're working on it.

AIFRED isn't a one-click mix generator, and it isn't here to throw presets at your track and pretend that fixed something. It measures the audio that's actually coming through the plugin, breaks down what it's seeing, and gives you useful information so you can decide what needs to change.

The Halo gives you a quick visual fingerprint of the mix — tone, stereo width, loudness, punch, dynamics, and how everything lines up against a reference. The meters are built around real DSP measurements instead of bouncing numbers around just to look active.

With AIFRED, you can look at:

  • tonal balance
  • stereo width and mono compatibility
  • loudness, peaks, and dynamics
  • punch and density
  • reference alignment
  • A/B comparison between your mix and a target

The whole point is to stop bouncing between five different meters trying to figure out what they're all telling you. AIFRED pulls that information together, gives you the bigger picture, and helps you figure out where to look next.

Less guessing. Better decisions. Better mixes.

Why AIFRED Exists

Why AIFRED Exists

A lot of tools give you numbers. That doesn't always mean they give you clarity.

When you're deep in a session, the hardest part usually is not finding a plugin to throw on the chain. It's figuring out what actually needs attention first.

AIFRED helps you answer questions like:

  • Is this mix too narrow?
  • Is the top end pushing too hard?
  • Is it loud enough or just crushed?
  • Is it actually punching or just dense?
  • How far off is it from the reference?
  • What needs to be fixed first?
